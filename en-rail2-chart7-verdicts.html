Court sentences after major train disasters: who was actually tried?

Four major disasters: defendants were always operational staff

"In every major disaster, operational staff were tried; no managers or decision-makers were in the dock"

Upper Egypt train fire, 2002

Acquittal

Eleven junior and middle-ranking employees were referred to trial; the court acquitted them all

Al-Ayyat train collision, 2009

6 months – 3 years

Eight railway workers were convicted and given varying sentences

Manfalut bus, 2012

10 years

Level-crossing worker and block supervisor: 10 years each (48 pupils killed)

Ramses Station fire, 2019

3–15 years

Fourteen defendants had their appeals rejected; sentences ranged from 3 to 15 years

⚠ In every case, the defendants were operational staff: no managers or decision-makers in the dock

Source: Court judgments; multiple press sources
